Snorkel. Adventure. Explore.

Full Day Guided Custom Snorkel Tour for up to 7 guests. Tell us where you want to explore, and we will take you there! (Exception is Anegada — ask for more info)

There are so many underwater wonders to explore here in the BVI. From the Indians and Treasure Caves of Norman Island, to the Coral Gardens of Dead Chest, and the Secret Bays of Sand Dollars and Sea Biscuits — natural beauty is all around, and it’s ours to explore for the day.

We enjoy taking our guests to awesome remote locations where we are usually the only ones exploring. That said, there are also nice establishments to go “Limin’” (hang out / chill) if your crew decides they’ve had enough snorkeling and just want to relax.

A typical custom full-day snorkel adventure consists of four snorkel spots and a lunch stop. We don’t provide lunch, but we do provide fresh fruit, a snack, juice/soft drinks, and a cooler full of ice in case you would like to bring your own drinks.
