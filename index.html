Frequently Asked Questions

How can I book a private boat trip?

The fastest way is WhatsApp at +1 307-381-8011. You can also email sunnydayadventurevi@gmail.com or use the form below. Tell us your preferred date, number of guests, and which tour.

What is included in the Private Full Day Snorkel Adventure?

Licensed local captain, top-quality snorkel gear, guided snorkel, juice/soda, healthy snacks, cold water, fully iced cooler (BYO drinks welcome), fuel, National Park fees and cruising permits. Lunch is not included.

Do you offer pickup from cruise ships or outer islands?

Yes. Road Town cruise pier pickup is available. Outer-island or resort pickups have a $100 fuel surcharge.

What should I bring?

Reef-safe sunscreen, towel, hat, swimsuit, and any personal snacks or drinks. We supply snorkel gear, water, soft drinks, ice and basic snacks.

What is your cancellation policy?

• 7+ days in advance: Full refund
• 3–7 days: 50% of the cost
• Less than 72 hours: 100% of the cost

How many people can join a private tour?

Full Day Snorkel: up to 7 guests. Essentials Boat Tour: up to 6 guests. Larger groups possible with advance notice.

Can we visit The Indians, Norman Island or The Baths?

Yes. Most full-day snorkel itineraries include The Indians and Treasure Caves. The Essentials tour can include The Baths, Cooper Island, Norman Island or Peter Island.
